## Product Photography

A professional product photograph of [object], isolated on a [color] background.
Studio lighting: softbox from [direction], reflector fill.
Camera: macro lens, sharp focus on [key detail].

Composition: centered, slight angle to show [dimensions/features].
Materials visible: [describe materials and textures].

Style: clean commercial photography, high-end product shot.
Resolution: 8K, ultra-detailed.

Negative prompt: reflections, shadows on background, distortion, low quality

---

## Food Photography

A mouth-watering food photograph of [dish name].
Presentation: [plating style], [garnish details].
Lighting: natural window light from the side, soft shadows.
Angle: [45-degree / overhead / eye-level].

Background: [rustic wood / marble / dark slate], shallow depth of field.
Colors: vibrant and appetizing, [specific color notes].

Negative prompt: unappetizing, messy, plastic-looking, artificial

## Object with Context

A [object] placed in a [setting/scene].
Surroundings: [describe environment and supporting props].
Lighting matches the scene: [describe lighting].

The object is the focal point, composition draws the eye to it.
Style: [cinematic / editorial / lifestyle photography].

Negative prompt: cluttered, object too small, out of focus
