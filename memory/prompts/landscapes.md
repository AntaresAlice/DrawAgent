# Landscape Prompt Templates

tags: landscape, scenery, nature, environment, background

---

## Natural Landscape

A breathtaking landscape of [scene type: mountain/lake/forest/ocean/desert].
[Season/time of day], [weather condition].
Composition: [foreground element] in the foreground, [midground element] in the middle,
[background element] in the distance.

Lighting: [golden hour / overcast / sunset / moonlight].
Atmosphere: [misty / clear / dramatic clouds].

Style: National Geographic photograph, ultra-wide angle.
Technical: 8K resolution, highly detailed, sharp focus throughout.

Negative prompt: blurry, low resolution, people, man-made structures

---

## Cityscape / Urban

An urban cityscape of [city name or style], [time of day].
Architecture style: [modern / gothic / cyberpunk / traditional].
Viewpoint: [bird's-eye / street level / rooftop].

Foreground: [describe street level details].
Background: [skyline description, prominent buildings].
Atmosphere: [neon lights / fog / rain / golden sunset].

Negative prompt: distorted buildings, empty streets, low quality

---

## Interior Scene

An interior scene of [room type], [style: cozy/modern/minimalist/victorian].
Furniture: [key furniture pieces].
Lighting: [natural window light / warm lamps / candlelight].
Color palette: [dominant colors].

Details: [textures, materials, decorations].
Mood: [peaceful / dramatic / mysterious].

Negative prompt: empty room, distorted furniture, bad proportions, dark
