# Portraits Prompt Templates

tags: portrait, face, character, person, human

---

## Standard Portrait

A professional portrait photograph of [subject], [expression], [lighting setup].
Shot with a [focal length] lens on [camera], shallow depth of field.
Background: [describe background].

Key elements:
- Focus on the eyes, ensure catchlights are visible
- Natural skin texture, avoid plastic/airbrushed look
- Clothing: [describe outfit]
- Pose: [describe pose]

Negative prompt: distorted face, asymmetric eyes, blurry, extra limbs, ugly, deformed

---

## Character Portrait (Fantasy)

A detailed fantasy character portrait of [character description].
[Race/species], [age], [distinctive features].
Wearing [armor/clothing description].
Expression: [expression].
Setting: [environment context].

Style: [art style — oil painting / digital art / concept art].
Lighting: dramatic rim light from [direction], ambient [color] fill.

Negative prompt: modern clothing, photo, deformed hands, extra fingers

---

## Character Sheet / Turnaround

A character reference sheet showing [character] from three angles:
front view, side view, and back view.
Full body, standing pose, neutral expression.
Clean white background, even lighting.

Style: game character concept art, clean linework.

Negative prompt: different poses, inconsistent design, shadows on background
