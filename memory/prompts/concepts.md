## Abstract Art

An abstract artwork exploring the concept of [theme: chaos/order/emotion/nature/technology].
Dominant colors: [color palette].
Technique: [acrylic pour / digital generative / watercolor wash / geometric].

Composition: [describe arrangement — flowing / structured / explosive].
Mood: [energetic / serene / mysterious / melancholic].

Style: contemporary abstract art, gallery quality.

Negative prompt: representational, realistic, photo, obvious subject

---

## Mood / Atmosphere

An atmospheric scene conveying [mood/emotion].
No specific subject required — focus on color, light, and composition.

Color palette: [describe colors and their emotional associations].
Light: [describe light quality — diffused, harsh, colored, absent].
Texture: [describe surface qualities].

Reference styles: [artist names or movements for inspiration].

Negative prompt: realistic, specific objects, people, text

---

## Conceptual Illustration

A conceptual illustration representing [idea/concept].
Visual metaphor: [describe the metaphorical visual element].
Style: [editorial illustration / surrealism / symbolic / minimalist].

Composition: [describe layout and visual hierarchy].
Symbolism: [explain key symbols and their meanings].

Negative prompt: literal interpretation, photo, cartoon, comic style
