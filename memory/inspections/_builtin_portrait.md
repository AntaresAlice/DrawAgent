# Built-in Portrait Inspection Checklist

tags: portrait, face, human, character

---

## Portrait-Specific Quality Checks

Use these in addition to the common checks.

### Face & Features
- Are both eyes the same size, shape, and color?
- Is the iris/pupil centered and round (not oval or distorted)?
- Are the catchlights in both eyes consistent with the light source?
- Is the nose properly shaped and centered?
- Is the mouth natural (teeth not fused, lips symmetrical)?
- Are ears the same shape and at the same height?
- Is the facial expression natural and matching the prompt?

### Hair
- Does the hairstyle match the description?
- Is the hair rendering natural (no plastic or melted look)?
- Is the hairline natural?
- Is the hair color consistent?

### Hands & Fingers (if visible)
- Count the fingers on each visible hand (should be 5 per hand).
- Are fingers proportional and naturally posed?
- Are there any fused or extra fingers?
- Are fingernails present and correctly placed?

### Body & Posture
- Is the body proportion correct (head-to-body ratio)?
- Are shoulders level and natural?
- Are limbs the correct length and thickness?
- Is the pose natural and matching the description?

### Skin & Texture
- Is the skin texture natural (not plastic or airbrushed)?
- Are there any seam lines or texture blending issues?
- Is the skin tone consistent across visible areas?
