# Built-in Common Inspection Checklist

tags: common, general, quality, basics

---

## Overall Image Quality

Check the image for general technical quality issues:

### Composition
- Is the main subject properly framed and in focus?
- Is the composition balanced (not tilted, no wasted space)?
- Does the crop or framing feel intentional?

### Lighting & Exposure
- Is the lighting consistent (matching light sources and shadows)?
- Is the exposure correct (no blown highlights or crushed blacks)?
- Are colors natural and well-saturated (unless intentionally stylized)?

### Resolution & Details
- Is the image sharp where it should be?
- Are textures and fine details properly rendered?
- Is there any unwanted noise or compression artifacts?

### AI Artifacts (Universal)
- Are there any distorted or extra limbs (especially hands)?
- Are faces and eyes symmetrical and correctly proportioned?
- Are there any warped or melting textures?
- Are repeating patterns consistent (bricks, tiles, fabric patterns)?
- Check for floating or disconnected body parts.
- Check for impossible geometry or perspective errors.
