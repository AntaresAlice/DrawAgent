# Built-in Scene Inspection Checklist

tags: scene, landscape, environment, composition

---

## Scene-Specific Quality Checks

### Composition & Layout
- Is the scene properly framed (horizon level, rule of thirds)?
- Are foreground, midground, and background clearly defined?
- Is the depth of field appropriate for the scene type?
- Are leading lines or visual flow elements present?

### Architecture & Structures
- Are buildings structurally sound (no impossible geometry)?
- Are windows, doors, and architectural details consistent?
- Are repeating elements (windows, columns, arches) uniform?
- Is the perspective correct (vanishing points consistent)?

### Nature Elements
- Are trees and plants properly structured (trunk → branches → leaves)?
- Is foliage natural-looking (not repetitive pattern blobs)?
- Is water rendering realistic (reflections, ripples, transparency)?
- Are clouds natural (not smeared or repeated)?
- Are mountains/hills properly scaled relative to the scene?

### Atmospheric Effects
- Is fog/mist rendering naturally (not just a white overlay)?
- Are light rays/volumetric effects realistic?
- Is the weather effect (rain, snow, etc.) consistent throughout?
- Is the sky color consistent with the time of day?

### Scale & Proportion
- Are objects properly scaled relative to each other?
- Is the sense of scale conveyed (epic vs intimate)?
- Are there any perspective inconsistencies?
